喵阅 Nyan Read · Reader Chrome — Design Direction

One Paper.

The reader chrome read like two apps stitched together: a flat, edge-to-edge control bar fighting a floating, high-radius settings card. This is the redesign — a single material, a single light, a single radius family. Every bar, sheet, and button is now cut from the same sheet of paper, and the control bar doesn't switch into the settings sheet — it grows into it.

The cat sat on the threshold for a long while, watching the rain darken the stones and the wisteria release its scent into the late spring evening. Outside the gate, somewhere across the river, a temple bell struck once — slow, deliberate, the sort of sound that asks nothing of you.

The doctrine

One material, one light, one radius family.

Nyan Read's manifesto is "a book, not an app — Muji × paper × restrained matcha." The old chrome broke that promise: the control bar behaved like an Android system toolbar while the sheets behaved like iOS cards. The fix isn't a re-skin — it's a single law that every container now obeys.

01

Everything floats, nothing is welded

No chrome touches the screen edge. Every bar, sheet, and panel insets 12px from all sides and lifts on one shadow. The hard, edge-to-edge bar is gone.

02

The bar and the sheet are one object

The control bar is the collapsed state of the settings panel — same width, same inset, same surface, same corners. Opening settings doesn't swap views; the panel grows upward and the toolbar stays as its footer.

03

Matcha is the only voice raised

One strong colour does all the talking: active tab, progress fill, selected outline, the current chapter. Everything else is cream and warm ink. Orange is reserved for warmth only.

Colour harmony

Cream paper, warm ink, one matcha.

No pure white, no pure black. Surfaces step by tone, never by border — background to muted to surface. Layering is done with colour steps, not glass or gradient.

Typography

Five sizes, three weights. No exceptions.

UI sets in Noto Sans SC; the reading canvas may switch to a CJK serif. The scale is closed — if you reach for a sixth size, restructure the hierarchy instead.

Corner radius

A concentric family, never random.

Radius scales with elevation and nests concentrically: a card inside a sheet is rounded just enough that its arc sits parallel to the parent's. The resting dock is 24; when it grows into a sheet it eases to 28. Nothing is square; nothing is edge-to-edge but the page.

Spacing

An 8-point grid with a 12px chrome inset.

Steps are 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 — 10, 14, 18 are banned. Card padding is always 16. The single dimension that escapes the grid is the 44px minimum tap target, applied to everything tappable.

Iconography

One family. One stroke. No mixing.

The old chrome mixed thin Material outlines with a circle-backed matcha play button. Now everything is Phosphor Regular at a constant ~1.5px stroke. Fill weight is reserved for exactly two states — a set bookmark and the selected check. Nothing else fills.

PHOSPHOR REGULAR · FILL RESERVED FOR 2 STATES

Depth & focus

Three elevations, one response.

Depth was the missing layer — bars stacked with no sense of what sat above what. Now there are exactly three tiers, and a single, predictable response when a sheet rises: the page dims under a 34% ink scrim, recedes to 0.97 scale, and softens. No frosted glass — paper, not iOS. Text can never bleed through.

Component 01

The Bottom Control Bar → the Dock.

It stops pretending to be a system toolbar. The dock is a floating paper tile: progress and percent on top, three Phosphor actions below, the active one in matcha. Try it on the device — tap the page, then tap an action.

BEFORE
  • Edge-to-edge, hard top border
  • Thin Material icons + a circle play button
  • Flat — no relationship to the sheets
  • Four cramped actions, no hierarchy
AFTER
  • Floats, inset 12px, radius 24 + soft lift
  • One Phosphor family, 24px, ~1.5px stroke
  • Literally the sheet, collapsed
  • Progress + 3 actions, matcha = active

Component 02

The Settings / Chapters Sheet.

The heavy filled-green sort button and the mismatched card radii are gone. The sheet shares the dock's exact width and inset, so opening it reads as one panel unfolding — the toolbar stays pinned as the footer and the action you tapped stays lit. Inside, two selection patterns and nothing else: a recessed segmented control for top-level views, and the signature outline-selected pill for every option chip.

Sheets vs. destinations. A sheet is for transient adjustments to this page — settings, the chapter jump. Anything that is a place you go to and browse is a destination, not a sheet: Bookmarks now pushes a full page (slides in, back arrow returns you), so it can grow a real list without straining a sheet. The rule: adjust → sheet; browse → page.

BEFORE
  • Appears with no link to the bar you tapped
  • Heavy filled-green pill ≠ the settings tabs
  • Circle number badges, ad-hoc radii
  • No scrim — text bled through behind it
AFTER
  • Grows from the dock; footer stays lit
  • One segmented control for sort and tabs
  • Concentric 16px cards, 12px chips
  • 34% scrim + page recede keeps focus
