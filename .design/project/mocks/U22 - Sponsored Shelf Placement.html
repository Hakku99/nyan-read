How a sponsored placement lives on the free Public Shelf without cheapening it — shown in list and grid. Option B is the served-ad case: a clearly-labelled Discover block anchored below the user’s own books, so it never interrupts the shelf. Option C is the no-ad case: with no placement to serve, the slot becomes a first-party Pro nudge instead. Both keep the One-Paper calm and are always dismissible.
