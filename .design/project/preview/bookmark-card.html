Bookmark card — U12 Bookmark List
Uppercase primary-deep eyebrow, a 2-line excerpt, and — when the bookmark carries a note — a hairline divider followed by a Note pill and the note text. (In the list, a swipe-left rail reveals Delete; that rail belongs to the list context, not the card.)
Bookmark 1
She would sometimes say that of all the noises in the city, only the bell and the rain felt honest.
Note
Opens and closes the chapter — the bell is the thesis.
Bookmark 2
He set the lacquer cup down on the low table and looked, again, at the calligraphy.
