What changed from the previous draft
Two complaints from review: "the cat mascot looks ugly, totally not similar with sample provided on the right" and "color theme is not matching current design system." Both addressed.
Wide chubby head
Old: tall narrow head, long pointed ears reaching y = 36 on a 200 box. New: head fills x = 40 – 160 with small rounded ear bumps — proportions read kawaii at a glance.
Matcha sage body
Old draft was cream-on-cream — invisible against the page. New body is sage #B5C29A (tint of --nyan-primary-src) — matches the production PNG and reads against the paper background.
Matcha book + cream pages
Old draft used a translucent pink book — off-palette. New book uses the production matcha cover (--nyan-primary-deep-src) with cream pages, a fish doodle on the right page, and two cream paws resting on the spine.
Tail + heart + flicks
Three kawaii punctuation marks, kept from the reference. Pink heart (warmth) on the left, three short flicks (excitement) on the right, sage tail curling out to the right of the book.
Style commitments
  • Matcha-first, exactly like the production PNG. Cat body is #B5C29A — a lighter tint of --nyan-primary-src (#98A27C). Book cover is --nyan-primary-deep-src (#7E8B61). The wordmark stays brown ink. No new color tokens.
  • Kawaii proportions from the reference. Wide chubby head (x = 40 – 160 on the 200 box) with two small rounded ear bumps instead of long pointed triangles. Dot eyes with a single cream highlight pixel — the simplest readable kawaii eye.
  • Cream whiskers. Old whiskers were brown-on-cream — they're now cream-on-sage, the only correct contrast direction once the body is green.
  • One pink accent, on purpose. The heart is the only non-matcha color on the badge. It's there for warmth and to hint at the highlight-pen pink in the larger design system — without dragging the whole mark away from the brand's matcha center of gravity.
  • One pose, two scales. The full mark carries the story (peeking, book, fish, heart, speed lines). The compact face-only mark carries the silhouette down to 16 px. Same character, different responsibilities.

Splash composition
  • Background: --nyan-bg (#F6F3EA) full bleed.
  • Mark: 128 × 128 dp, vertically centered on the device safe area. The badge frame becomes the app-icon outline.
  • 喵阅: Source Han Serif SC SemiBold, 26 sp, letter-spacing: 6.
  • Nyan Read: Noto Sans SC Medium, 12 sp, all-caps, letter-spacing: 4, --nyan-text-secondary.
  • Tagline: --nyan-text-muted, 11 sp, 32 dp from bottom safe area.
  • No motion. If anything moves, fade-in the mark over 240 ms ease-out-cubic — nothing else.

Usage rules
  • Use as-is. The mark is a flat illustration with baked-in fills — don't try to re-tint individual strokes or fills.
  • Clear-space around the badge = one ear-height (~14 % of the badge edge). Nothing crosses that ring, including the heart and speed-line accents.
  • Minimum sizes: Standalone ≥ 64 px, Compact ≥ 16 px. Below 16 px, use a typographic 喵 glyph instead.
  • Never recolor, rotate, skew, or place on photography. The matcha discipline is what makes the cozy atmosphere work — saturated backgrounds or photo backdrops fight it.
  • For dark surfaces (sumi dark theme, marketing on dark), use the Compact mark inverted — set its background to a dark warm-ink and the body fill to cream. The full mark is not designed for dark surfaces; if you need one, request a dark variant rather than improvising.
