Recommendation: Phosphor Regular
The product reads quieter with a thinner stroke — paper books don't shout. Phosphor Regular keeps every glyph crisp at 16–24 pt, has the same coverage Material does (~1300 icons), and pairs better with the Noto Sans SC ink weight. Phosphor Light is even sleeker but falls below the WCAG 3:1 non-text-contrast minimum on cream surface at small sizes — use it for ≥24 pt only.
Tradeoff to know: The Flutter source uses Icons.*_rounded (Material Round). Switching this design system to Phosphor means designs you make here won't be pixel-identical to live screenshots of the running app. For marketing comps / new design work this is fine. For UI bug repros or screenshot annotations, stay on Material Round (or load both and use Material in the kit, Phosphor in marketing).

Loading either set:
Material Round (current): <link rel="stylesheet" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/icon?family=Material+Icons+Round">
Phosphor: <link rel="stylesheet" href="https://unpkg.com/@phosphor-icons/web@2.1.1/src/regular/style.css"> then <i class="ph ph-book-open"></i>

Other free sets I considered: Lucide (MIT, ~1.5px) reads almost identical to Phosphor Regular but with fewer rounded terminals — slightly more "tech," less "paper." Heroicons (MIT) is excellent but its 24×24 only style limits sizing. Iconoir (MIT) is closest in calmness but ~1300 icons (vs Phosphor's 1300+ in 6 weights).
