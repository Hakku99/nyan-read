Nesting rule — concentric corners
When a rounded shape sits inside another rounded shape, the corners must stay concentric — the stroke gap between parent and child must be uniform all the way around. Otherwise the child either bulges past the parent's curve (bad) or sits inside it lopsidedly. Formula:
child_radius = parent_radius − padding

Outer card · 20  −  padding 4  =  inner input · 16
✗ BULGE

Exceptions
Option chips — chips are r-chip · 12, squared to match every other container (no longer stadium pills). When a chip sits inside a Knob card the concentric gap is read visually, not computed.
Circles — the matcha selected-check badge, the bookmark indicator dot, and slider thumbs. Treat radius as 50%; no nesting math applies.
Nothing welds to the edge — every bar, dock, and sheet floats inset 12 px and is rounded on all four corners. The reader dock rests at dock · 24 and eases to sheet · 28 as it grows. Only the reading canvas itself reaches the device edge.
