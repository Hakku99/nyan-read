Rules
  • No tile chrome. The card has no background, border, or shadow of its own — it sits directly on the shelf. Structure comes from the cover wash, not a container.
  • Cover ratio is fixed at 120 : 156. Width is set by the 3-column grid; never hard-code a card width.
  • Format chip (EPUB / TXT / PDF) pins to the cover's top-right in a translucent surface pill.
  • Progress bar appears only when pct > 0 — unread books skip it, so the title sits directly under the cover.
  • Title clamps to 2 lines, author to 1 (ellipsis). Rows stay even because the cover — not the text — drives height.
  • Selected state (U21): a 2 px primary ring + 3 px halo on the cover and a top-left check. Selection chrome lives on the cover, never the text.
